Back Then

Clickable prototype for iPhone and Android. Each phone runs on its own: go through onboarding, open the calendar, open a photo and heart it, select a few and make an album, then share the album link.

Decisions so far

Quiet, photo-first UI. Neutral surfaces, one coral accent kept for hearts, selection and the current day.

The calendar picks a month and day, not a year, because every year is shown at once. Dots mark days that have photos.

Same app on both platforms. Chrome follows each one: floating tab bar and sheets on iPhone, navigation bar and a centered date dialog on Android. Apple Photos leads on iPhone, Google Photos on Android.

Photos are random stand-ins.

Use Tweaks to switch dark mode, skip onboarding, or try the year-by-year strip layout.
